**Concept and 3D mapping video for the 10-th birthday of Hilton, Sofia.**
**Концепция и**

**3D мапинг видео за 10-тия рожден ден на Хилтън, София**

On the evening of 10.11.2011 the 10-years old open-book-like building in the center of Sofia was again "opened" with light 3d mapping show and fireworks.
Past, Present and Future -- the guiding line - with the desired by the client Wow effect, without being boring, and without the clichés of 3D mapping were the leading aspects by which Arch-i-Tech Ltd. prepared the concept and designed the lighting show.
The Past -- that is how and the foyer/Entry of the hotel is designed as an ancient-temple-like architecture. The Present -- the green, bio-ecological experiments of Hilton to breed bees and produce honey on its roof with remarkable success. The Future -- stepping in the second decade of its history with bright ideas.
