Created for Chezic LLC. Tsupod helps people stay safe, stay alive and keeps them afloat during tsunamis and floods. A practical, compact and affordable life saving device.

Community version comes with 2 entry hatches and 1 ramp door. Ideal for public places near the coastal communities.
