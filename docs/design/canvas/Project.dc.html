Lagoon as spine

Ocean Lake Resort sits one block from the Atlantic, organized around a single lagoon that runs the full length of the site, crossed at its midpoint by a new pedestrian bridge. The water is the spine: residential lots line both shores, a condo-hotel and mixed-use building anchor the lagoon mouth nearest the beach, and floating docks distribute kayaks and paddleboards along the full waterfront.

Existing structures — the swimming pool, pickleball courts, and gym/spa — are retained and renovated rather than demolished, folding the resort's prior footprint into the new master plan.

Hospitality & open space

Three 18-unit condo-hotel buildings anchor the lagoon mouth nearest the beach, paired with a four-story mixed-use building of ground-floor retail and dining beneath upper-level units. Rooftop terraces and a top-floor restaurant overlook both the lagoon and the Atlantic horizon.

Over five acres of gardens span seven nodes — sports fields, a bridge garden with pickleball, a playground and continuous bike and jogging paths — with eleven floating docks giving every lot direct water access.
